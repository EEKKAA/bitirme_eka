How the Model Works — The Core Idea

In one sentence: the model reads a company's financial statements, adds Turkey's macroeconomic context, and outputs a probability that the firm will fall into financial distress within the next year.

📊

1. INPUT — Firm Data

Balance sheet, income statement & cash flow of a BIST firm for year t

🌐

2. INPUT — Macro

Inflation, credit growth, unemployment, GDP growth — Turkey's economic climate in year t

🧩

3. TRANSFORM

Raw numbers become 35 features: 22 ratios, 4 trends, 3 macro-firm interactions, 4 macro, 2 auxiliary

🧠

4. MODEL — CatBoost

Gradient-boosted trees learn distress patterns from 2,229 historical firm-years

🚨

5. OUTPUT — Decision

Probability in [0, 1]. If p ≥ 0.60, the firm is flagged as distress risk for year t+1

What does the model actually learn?

It learns which combinations of financial weakness (low retained earnings, negative equity, liquidity stress) and macro pressure (high interest rates, credit contraction) historically preceded TTK 376 capital impairment. No hand-crafted rules — patterns are discovered from data.

Why is this useful?

Creditors, auditors, and regulators get a one-year-ahead early warning signal grounded in Turkish legal reality (TTK 376/2 & 376/3), explainable via SHAP, and tested on unseen companies — not just unseen years.

The Panel Dataset

A comprehensive firm-year panel constructed from KAP (Public Disclosure Platform) filings of BIST-listed companies covering 7 fiscal years.

Dataset Profile

  • 2,229 firm-year observations from 331 unique BIST companies.
  • 70.9% (1,580) Healthy firm-year periods.
  • 29.1% (649) Financially distressed periods.
  • 25 financial line items extracted per company per year from balance sheet and income statement.
  • 9 macroeconomic indicators merged at the annual level.

Class ratio 1:2.4 — moderate imbalance handled via StratifiedGroupKFold ensuring company-level integrity across folds.

Labeling Strategy

Financial distress is defined using the Turkish Commercial Code (TTK) Articles 376/2 and 376/3 — the legal framework for capital impairment.

  • TTK 376/2: Total equity falls below 1/3 of paid-in capital. The board must convene a general assembly to consider remediation measures.
  • TTK 376/3: Total equity falls below 2/3 of paid-in capital, or the company becomes insolvent (liabilities exceed assets). This triggers mandatory dissolution proceedings.
  • Healthy (0): Companies that satisfy neither condition across the entire observation window.
  • Label smoothing: Isolated single-year distress episodes are removed to reduce label noise (688 → 649 distress observations).

Formula: Equity < Paid_Capital × (1/3)  ⇒  Distress = 1

Feature Engineering

A 35-feature topology across five dimensions, designed to capture both internal financial health and external macroeconomic pressures.

  • 22 Financial Ratios across 5 categories: Capital Structure (6), Liquidity (3), Profitability (5), Activity (3), Solvency & Cash Flow (5).
  • 4 Trend Features — 1-year momentum (delta) for critical ratios detecting deterioration speed.
  • 3 Interaction Terms — Micro-macro cross-features linking firm state to economic environment.
  • 4 Macro Indicators — Direct effects: inflation, credit growth, unemployment, lagged GDP growth.
  • 2 Auxiliary Features — Firm size control (log total assets) and first-year flag.

SelectKBest (f_classif, k=18) selects the top 18 features from the 35-feature pool via ANOVA F-test within each CV fold.

Why ratios instead of raw numbers?

Ratios are scale-free — a small firm and a large holding become directly comparable. They normalize away firm size and sector effects.

Why these five categories?

Each category addresses a different failure mode: over-leverage, illiquidity, unprofitability, inefficient asset use, or cash-flow starvation.

End-to-End ML Pipeline

A fully reproducible, leakage-free pipeline with company-level cross-validation and out-of-fold threshold optimization.

📁

1. Data Collection

25 financial items from KAP filings + 9 macro indicators (TCMB, TUIK)

⚙

2. Feature Eng.

22 ratios + 4 trends + 3 interactions + 4 macro + 2 auxiliary = 35 features

🔀

3. Preprocessing

Train-only imputation, winsorization (1st-99th), MinMaxScaler per fold

🎯

4. Selection

SelectKBest (f_classif, k=18) ANOVA F-test inside each fold

🧠

5. Training

10-fold StratifiedGroupKFold + RandomizedSearchCV (20 iter)

No Data Leakage

Imputation medians computed from training set only. All preprocessing fitted inside each CV fold.

Company Integrity

StratifiedGroupKFold ensures all years of a company remain in the same fold — no temporal leakage.

OOF Threshold

Optimal classification threshold determined from 2,229 out-of-fold predictions — not from test data.

SHAP Explainability

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) reveals the contribution of each feature to individual predictions, ensuring model transparency.

  • Dominant Feature: retained_earnings_to_assets has a mean |SHAP| of 4.098 — 7× stronger than any other feature. Cumulative profitability across years is the single strongest distress signal a firm can send.
  • Capital structure matters: equity_to_assets ranks #2, confirming solvency ratios are critical.
  • Macro signals are strong: Inflation, unemployment, and credit growth all appear in the top 10, validating the macro integration approach.
  • Interactions work: wc_ta_x_credit (working capital x credit growth) ranks #8, proving micro-macro synergy.

Threshold Optimization

Instead of using the default 0.50 cutoff, we find the optimal probability threshold that maximizes F1 score on unbiased out-of-fold predictions.

  • Default t = 0.50: F1 = 0.8583, which already captures most distressed firms but misses borderline cases.
  • OOF Optimal t = 0.60: F1 improves to 0.8692 by reducing false positives. The model is highly confident in its distress predictions.
  • Why t > 0.50? The model's predicted probabilities are well-calibrated. Raising the threshold cuts noise from uncertain predictions near the boundary.

Key Contributions

  • TTK 376-based labeling — First study using Turkish legal capital impairment criteria as the ground truth for distress.
  • Comprehensive ratio coverage — 22 financial ratios across 5 dimensions, including solvency & cash flow metrics often overlooked in prior work.
  • Macro-micro interactions — Novel feature engineering linking firm ratios to Turkey's high-inflation and credit-tightening environment.
  • Leakage-free pipeline — Company-level CV splits, train-only imputation, and out-of-fold threshold optimization.

Practical Impact

  • Early warning system for regulators (SPK/BIST) to flag companies before capital erosion becomes critical.
  • Credit risk assessment tool for banks evaluating corporate loan portfolios.
  • Investor protection — identifying structurally weak companies before share price collapse.
  • SHAP transparency — every prediction is explainable, meeting regulatory audit requirements.
