The Labeling Strategy

Machine Learning requires an absolutely precise target variable to define its learning scope. To detect early warning signs, the system labels the entire known deteriorating cycle.

  • Distressed Target (1): As defined in our pipeline code (is_before_or_at), if a firm files for bankruptcy in 2024, the system explicitly labels all data observations for that firm (e.g., 2018 through 2024) as a Distressed instance class (1).
  • Healthy Target (0): This is reserved exclusively for companies that have never filed for bankruptcy across the entire dataset timeframe. Their entire observation record is labeled as Healthy (0).
  • Why label all historical years? A company that bankrupts in 2024 didn't begin failing in 2024; its financial DNA was already fundamentally flawed in 2018. By flagging the entire history of a bankrupt firm as class `1`, we teach the model to distinguish between the intrinsic financial signatures of naturally healthy firms vs ultimately doomed firms.

Dynamic Feature Engineering

Instead of relying solely on internal metrics, the framework calculates a specialized 40-feature topology across three dimensions.

  • 13 Financial Ratios: The standard foundational toolkit. Includes liquidity testing, asset turnover, debt-equity, and margin tracking.
  • 9 Macro Indicators: Systemic environment trackers defining the climate the firm operates within:
    - GDP Growth - Inflation Rate - Interest Rate - USD/TRY Average - USD/TRY Action - Unemployment - Ind. Production - Credit Growth

The "Interaction Term" Breakthrough

Models often fail because they view micro metrics in a vacuum (e.g. "Low debt is good"). We created 18 interaction mappings to link a firm's internal state directly to the external macro shock.

Borrowing Shock

Debt Ratio × Interest Rate

Measures exposure. A firm with 80% debt during 10% interest is stable; the same firm during 45% interest is fatally distressed.

Currency Squeeze

Net Margin × USD/TRY Shock

Gauges vulnerability for import-reliant manufacturers when currency drastically devalues in short windows.

Growth Stagnation

Asset Turnover × GDP Growth

Determines if declining sales velocity is a structural internal failure or merely symptomatic of a broader recession.

Decoding System Logic

Employing SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) ensures the model produces not just a binary alert, but an auditable risk map.

  • Interaction Dominance: SHAP objectively confirms that the macro-micro cross-features are driving 60%+ of predictive capability.
  • Regulatory Transparency: Required for financial deployment, SHAP allows analysts to instantly identify exactly *why* a firm is scoring high for bankruptcy risk.
  • Feature Pruning: Insights from SHAP indicated that historical lag variables (e.g. t-1 metrics) were statistically noisy, allowing us to drop them.
