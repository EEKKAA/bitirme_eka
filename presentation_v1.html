The "Interaction Term" Breakthrough

Models often fail because they view micro metrics in a vacuum (e.g. "Low debt is good"). We created 18 interaction mappings to link a firm's internal state directly to an external macro shock.

Borrowing Defensibility

Debt Ratio × Interest Rate

Measures exposure. A firm with 80% debt during 10% interest is somewhat stable; the same firm during 45% interest is fatally distressed.

Currency Squeeze

Net Margin × USD/TRY

Gauges vulnerability for import-reliant manufacturers when domestic currency drastically devalues in short cyclical windows.

The "Interaction Term" Breakthrough

Further exploring the custom variables that define the BIST market reality.

Growth Stagnation

Asset Turnover × GDP Growth

Determines if declining sales velocity is a structural internal failure or merely symptomatic of a broader national recession.

Pricing Power (Inflation Factor)

Net Margin × Inflation

Demonstrates a company's ability to seamlessly transmit inflating supply costs to their end consumer to preserve their exact margin.

Validation & Anti-Leakage Strategy

Preventing data leakage and overfitting in financial AI is critical.

Cross-Validation

We used 10-Fold Stratified CV to ensure models are tested on entirely unseen firm-years.

Class Imbalance (SMOTE Constraint)

We apply SMOTE synthetic generation exclusively to the training folds. The testing set retains the true 28% natural distress imbalance. We don't test the model in an artificial sandbox.
